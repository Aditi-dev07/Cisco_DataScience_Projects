## 🎨 Design Philosophy
My work follows a **"Data-to-Ink Ratio"** optimization strategy:
* **Remove Clutter:** Eliminating unnecessary chart spines, ticks, and legends to reduce cognitive load.
* **Direct Labeling:** Placing data labels at the end of lines or on top of bars for immediate recognition.
* **Contextual Benchmarks:** Always including a "human-scale" reference point (like a Jalapeño or a human breath-hold) to make large numbers relatable.
* **Strategic Color:** Using color only to highlight the "Main Story" while keeping background data neutral.
