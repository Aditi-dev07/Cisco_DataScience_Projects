## 🧬 **Primate Research**
### **Tracing the Rise of Scientific Interest in Primates Through Language**

---

### **🔍 Project Overview**
Primates—our closest evolutionary relatives—have long fascinated scientists, anthropologists, and the public alike. Over the 20th century, advances in evolutionary biology, genetics, and behavioral science dramatically expanded primate research.

This project examines a key question:

> **Can the growth of scientific research be observed through changes in language usage over time?**

By analyzing historical word-frequency trends for primate-related terms, this project explores how scientific attention and discovery are reflected in written language.

---

### **🎯 Project Objectives**
- Analyze long-term trends in the usage of primate-related terms  
- Identify periods of increased linguistic attention aligned with scientific progress  
- Demonstrate how research activity influences cultural and academic discourse  

---

### **🗂️ Data Description**
#### **Primary Dataset**
- **Source:** Historical English word-frequency corpus  
- **Focus Terms:** Primate-related keywords such as:
  - `chimpanzee`, `gorilla`, `orangutan`, `primate`
- **Structure:** Time-series frequency data spanning multiple centuries

---

### **🔧 Data Preprocessing**
- Filtered corpus to isolate primate-specific terms
- Normalized frequencies to ensure comparability across time
- Aligned time windows with known milestones in biological and anthropological research

---

### **🔬 Methodology**
This project applies **exploratory time-series analysis** with an emphasis on scientific context.

#### **Analytical Approach**
- Visualization of word-frequency trends over time
- Comparative analysis across multiple primate species
- Identification of inflection points corresponding to:
  - Advances in evolutionary theory
  - Growth of primatology as a scientific discipline

#### **Rationale**
Language often lags behind discovery—but once research reaches public awareness, it leaves measurable traces in written records.

---

### **📈 Visualizations & Analytical Evidence**
Visual outputs include:
- Line plots showing the rise of primate-related terms
- Cross-species comparisons highlighting differential growth patterns
- Clear temporal alignment with scientific milestones
