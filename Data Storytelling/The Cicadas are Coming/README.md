## 🦗 **Cicadas Are Coming**
### **Tracking Public Attention Around Periodical Cicada Emergences**

---

### **🔍 Project Overview**
Periodical cicadas are among the most unusual insects in North America, emerging en masse every **13 or 17 years**. These rare events generate intense—but temporary—public attention, media coverage, and curiosity.

This project explores a simple but revealing question:

> **Do rare natural events leave detectable spikes in language usage—and do those spikes fade just as quickly as the events themselves?**

By analyzing historical word-frequency data for cicada-related terms, this project examines how predictable biological events influence public discourse over time.

---

### **🎯 Project Objectives**
- Analyze word-frequency trends related to cicadas over long time horizons  
- Identify spikes in language usage corresponding to known cicada emergence years  
- Examine how quickly public attention rises and fades after rare natural events  

---

### **🗂️ Data Description**
#### **Primary Dataset**
- **Source:** Historical English word-frequency corpus  
- **Structure:** Time-series word-frequency data  

#### **Focus Terms**
- `cicada`
- Related insect terminology

#### **Reference Events**
- Known **13-year and 17-year cicada emergence cycles**

---

### **🔧 Data Preprocessing**
- Filtered corpus to isolate cicada-related terms  
- Normalized word frequencies for comparability across decades  
- Annotated known emergence years to align biological events with language trends  

---

### **🔬 Methodology**
This project applies **event-aligned exploratory time-series analysis**.

#### **Analytical Approach**
- Visualization of cicada word-frequency trends across multiple centuries  
- Overlay of known emergence cycles on language data  
- Comparative analysis of baseline vs emergence-year usage  

#### **Rationale**
Rare but predictable events provide a natural experiment for studying how attention spikes—and how quickly it dissipates.

---

### **📈 Visualizations & Narrative Evidence**
The analysis includes:
- Line plots of cicada-related word usage over time

### **🧭 Interpretation Focus**

Sharp spikes in word usage align closely with known cicada emergence years, followed by rapid declines. This pattern suggests that public attention toward cicadas is **event-driven rather than sustained**, peaking during emergences and fading soon after.

---

### **✅ Results & Key Insights**

* Cicada-related word usage shows **clear, recurring spikes** at regular intervals
* Peaks correspond strongly with documented emergence cycles
* Attention drops rapidly following each event, returning to baseline levels

**Key Insight:**
Public attention toward rare natural events is intense but fleeting—and language reflects that pattern with remarkable precision.
