
## **🐾 Data Storytelling: Uncovering History through Animal Word Trends**
### *Decoding 300 Years of Human History through Quantitative Linguistics*
## 🚀 The Mission

This repository is a digital archeology project. Using the **Google Collab**, I’ve built a suite of tools to visualize how cultural revolutions, ecological collapses, and scientific milestones are permanently etched into our written language.

---

## 📂 The Research Portfolio

### 🦁 [ Any Animal Except...](https://www.google.com/search?q=./any-animal-except-project.ipynb)

* **The Story:** The British Menagerie Era (1750–1835).
* **The Discovery:** Proved that **physical presence = linguistic frequency**. Animals that were "exhibitable" (Lions) saw a massive surge, while "difficult" animals (Pandas) remained invisible in literature.

### 🐠 [Coral Conundrum](https://www.google.com/search?q=./coral-conundrum.ipynb)

* **The Story:** Why did reef fish mentions crash in 1998?
* **The Discovery:** Explored "Digital Extinction"—how the launch of Google and the consolidation of the internet changed how we reference niche species.
  
### 🛠️ [Plotting Animal Words (The Foundation)](https://www.google.com/search?q=./plotting-animal-words-project.ipynb)

* **The Engine:** Development of the core `plot_animal()` function.
* **The Discovery:** Analyzed the **1830s Cow Spike**. Proved that urbanization and the "Penny Press" era forced a rise in agricultural discourse, turning a common animal into a frequent regulatory topic.

### 🧬 [Primate Research](https://www.google.com/search?q=./primate-research.ipynb)

* **The Story:** Testing the impact of Peter Singer’s *Animal Liberation*.
* **The Discovery:** Found the **"Primate Exception."** While mentions of dogs and cats in research dropped by 70%, primates became more central to the scientific narrative.

### 🦋 [Silent Spring](https://www.google.com/search?q=./silent-spring.ipynb)

* **The Story:** Tracking the "Pesticide Paradox."
* **The Discovery:** Data shows a shift from "pest eradication" (insects) to "wildlife preservation" (birds) following the 1944 release of DDT, mirroring the birth of the modern environmental movement.

  
### 🦗 [The Cicadas are Coming](https://www.google.com/search?q=./cicadas-are-coming-project.ipynb)

* **The Story:** Predicting media "buzz" through biological cycles.
* **The Discovery:** Quantitative validation revealed that while Brood X is famous, **Brood II** actually commanded the highest historical media attention between 1915-1970.

### ⚓ [ Warm Waters off Peru](https://www.google.com/search?q=./warm-waters-peru-project.ipynb)

* **The Story:** The Scarcity Hypothesis vs. El Niño.
* **The Discovery:** Proved that **scarcity drives discourse**. As fish catches dropped during El Niño, "Anchoveta" mentions spiked due to economic anxiety and professional concern.
